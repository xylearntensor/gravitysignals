Privacy Policy

Gravity Signals  ·  Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Signals (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Gravity Signals does not require you to create an account. The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal information about you.

2. Tracking and Advertising

The App does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

3. In-App Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase-related information is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy practices. The App may check purchase status to provide access to content you have purchased.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally send personal information to third-party services. Apple may process information as part of providing App Store, payment, or operating-system services, in accordance with Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from users, including children. If the App’s information practices change, this section will be updated as appropriate.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features or privacy practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page, with the “Last updated” date changed accordingly.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the App Store product page for Gravity Signals.
